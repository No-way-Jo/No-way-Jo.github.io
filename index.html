KeHE Forward Forecast — Trend Overstock Diagnosis

Live Tool ↗

127,506-unit overforecast identified · full marks, all 4 eval criteria

Fellowship client project. Diagnosed the root cause behind recurring overstock on a food distributor's trend-driven product launches: their demand forecast reacts to sales shifts after they happen rather than predicting the turn. Validated the pattern against real company data — on one brand, the forecast kept climbing for two periods past the actual sales peak, overforecasting by 127,506 units across the following six periods — and ruled out two competing explanations using the data itself. Designed and built the KeHE Forward Forecast tool, scoring a new product's hype-vs-durability pattern against a comparable product's lifecycle to recommend an early course-correction before overbuying into a fading trend.

Verifying AI output isn't optional

Caught a fabricated client list and a false acquisition claim generated by an AI tool during the Checkr project. The lesson stuck: AI is a second reader, not the author. Always verify against the real source before it goes anywhere near a client.

Window functions finally clicked

Spent a week drilling SQL window functions and CTEs. The shift that mattered: stop thinking row-by-row, start thinking in partitions. Makes reconciliation and ranking problems dramatically simpler.
